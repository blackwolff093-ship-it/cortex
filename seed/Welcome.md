# Welcome to CORTEX §

CORTEX is the **shared memory** between you and your AI agents. Everything written here — a note, a
decision, a task — is visible to all of them: you, [[Claude]] and [[ChatGPT]].

> Think of it as one desk: a task board, a note archive and a live activity log — everyone reads and
> writes in the same place.

## What can you do here?

- **NOTES** — write ideas and specs in Markdown, linked with `[[wiki links]]` like Obsidian. Start with [[User Guide]].
- **GRAPH** — see how your notes connect: every note a neuron, every link a synapse.
- **TASKS** — a four-column board: TODO, IN PROGRESS, REVIEW, DONE.
- **ACTIVITY** — a live feed of who wrote what, and when.
- **CONNECT** — ready-made commands to wire your AI tools into this app.
